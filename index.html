001 / ORIGIN

I am Anlo-Ewe, from Keta — a town in the Volta Region of Ghana built on a narrow strip of sand between two waters. To the north lies the Keta Lagoon, the largest in the country. To the south, the open Gulf of Guinea. In places, the land between them is only a few hundred metres wide.

Living between a lagoon and a sea teaches you about margins. The Atlantic has claimed whole streets of old Keta; the town rebuilt behind the sea defence and carried on. I think about that when I build systems — everything important runs on a narrow strip of ground, held by people who refuse to stop maintaining it.

002 / INTERESTS

Outside of work, I have varying interest in history specifically World War II, archaeology, astronomy and industrial design. Recently, this has grown into a specific focus on World War era aircraft and the tactics of aerial combat. I am an avid gamer and currently spend some of my time in the flight simulation community, specifically flying vintage fighter planes in Aces of Thunder. I have a long-term goal of eventually transitioning from flight simulators to piloting a Cessna in the real world.

003 / THE ARTS

I carry a standing love for the arts. I am drawn to work where the craft is visible — painting, sculpture, photography, architecture, and the design of everyday objects. Engineering and art solve the same problem from opposite ends: both are about making deliberate choices under constraint, and the best of either discipline looks inevitable once it exists.

004 / OFF HOURS

My free time splits between two ongoing collections. The first is tech toys — small machines and gadgets that don't strictly need to exist, built and tinkered with for the joy of it. The second is music — I collect it deliberately, hunting down albums and recordings across genres and decades. The "now listening" signal at the top of this page is a live feed from that habit.
